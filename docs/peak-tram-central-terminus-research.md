# Peak Tram Central Terminus: research and site constraints

The 2022 public entrance is at 33 Garden Road, beneath St John's Building, not at the currently mapped boarding stop. [Operator](https://www.thepeak.com.hk/en/getting-to-the-peak/location); [upgrade account](https://www.prc-magazine.com/the-peak-tram-upgrade-project/). [Gwulo](https://gwulo.com/node/18451) says the new platform was moved approximately 42 m uphill from the old one, below Cotton Tree Drive and above the Albany Nullah. OSM station area [way 636324517](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/636324517) and St John's office building [way 179091496](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/179091496) are mapping, not surveys. The present OSM stop is at 22.2771695, 114.1585770; the tower is around 22.2777, 114.1592. Do not place the platform immediately below the office tower.

## Appearance

References, attribution, local images and links: [central terminus reference folder](../references/hongkong/peak-tram/central-terminus/README.md). The street image shows a recessed dark entrance with red interior, curved grey stone sign band, landscaping/rough-stone retaining walls, chrome pillars and rounded grey office facade with dark rounded rectangular windows. The black reflecting pool holds Lindy Lee's vertically elongated *Eye of Infinity*: the [operator](https://www.thepeak.com.hk/sites/peak/files/2022-08/Peak%20Tram%20Unveils%20Stunning%20Public%20Artwork%20-%20Eye%20of%20Infinity%20by%20Lindy%20Lee_1.pdf) describes a 10 m cast bronze torus with black patina, numerous small openings and internal lights. The landscaping designer describes stonework, planting and a reflective pool at the entrance ([SCENIC](http://www.studioscenic.com/project/peak-tram-upgrade/)). The [platform lighting supplier](https://www.led-linear.com/gallery/the-peak-tram) identifies linear lighting along the platform wall. [Operator](https://www.thepeak.com.hk/sites/peak/files/2022-08/Announce%20Launch%20Date%20of%206th%20generation%20Peak%20Tram_ENG_FINAL.pdf) describes step-free level boarding and covered, temperature-controlled waiting; the station should not be represented as an open street-level tram shelter.

## Scene audit / unresolved integration

The unsurveyed joint profile currently starts at x=-105.43, z=523.83, rail y=39.22. At the same point `hongKongTerrainHeight` is about 47.08 and full road query returns Cotton Tree Drive road 720 at 47.3. A road above the stop is consistent with the sources, but the current solid terrain would bury a platform and rail, and the road is only about 8 m above this trial rail elevation. A correct model requires a local underground opening and road structure, a connected route from the lower street entrance, and an explicit height-aware clearance analysis. The scene's road/DTM/profile elevations are not surveys. Cutting a large rectangle or moving the road indiscriminately would damage traffic and surrounding terrain. The track from this platform to the Kennedy bridge is not yet built into the main scene.

This audit and image collection do not constitute a finished station. The photo-directed architecture, sculpture, walkable platform, night lighting, road and terrain junctions, and complete-game visual and vehicle testing remain to be built. The user's requirement that track be above its local ground surface still applies; overhead roads and covered station structures are separate layers, not a reason to bury the track in terrain.

Update: the separately modelled street entrance and simplified sculpture are described in `peak-tram-central-entrance.md`; the relocated platform and connection remain unfinished.

Update: provisional platforms and the static approach to Kennedy bridge now exist; see `peak-tram-central-platform.md`. A usable connection between the street entrance and these platforms remains absent.

## Additional engineering reference

The upgrade engineer [Arup](https://www.arup.com/en-us/projects/peak-tram-upgrade-project/) describes a roughly 40 m inclined moving-walkway link with an aluminium-clad spiral portal roof and skylights, not a straight 97 m ramp. The same page confirms the platform integrates a steep track gradient, automatic platform screen gates and air-conditioning docking; its platform photograph shows grey tiled flooring, tactile guidance and glazed barriers. The engineer's illustrations and photographs are copyrighted; direct source links are recorded in the reference folder rather than redistributing the images. Dimensions, position and heights of the moving walkway cannot be inferred from the illustration alone. The current 97 m scene gap includes an unmodelled waiting area and is not a measured length of the real walkway.
