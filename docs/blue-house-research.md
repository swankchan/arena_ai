# 灣仔藍屋 / Blue House

- The four-storey balcony-type tong lau at 72–74A Stone Nullah Lane is a Grade I historic building. [OpenStreetMap way 405793051 v5](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/way/405793051/full.json) supplies the source outline, including the irregular rear; the source is © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. The 15.2 m height is an architectural approximation rather than a surveyed elevation. The model is the Blue House itself, not a complete Yellow/Orange House cluster.
- [Panorama Streetline's restored street elevation](https://panoramastreetline.com/wan-chai-blue-house-hongkong-china-P19396) and [Wikimedia's earlier photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:HK_Wan_Chai_Stone_Nullah_Lane_Blue_House_SR.JPG) show the blue walls, three levels of projecting verandahs, pale brackets and slab edges, slim columns, ornamental iron balustrades and timber doors. The pale adjoining 74A unit is retained. Individual door and window sizes, paint, rail ornament, signage and roof height are photo-directed approximations; the signs include historical associations and are not a verified current directory.
- The model is an exterior; its shopfronts are not traversable and the original timber stairs, apartments and ground-floor interior are not represented. Blue House backs and neighbouring buildings are not surveyed.
