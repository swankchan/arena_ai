# Kennedy Road uphill track section

The unified scene now includes the approximately 74 m route from the uphill end of the Kennedy Road bridge to OSM node 6534947838, short of MacDonnell Road. This section uses the experimental joint profile, not surveyed levels. It has rails, sleepers, a track bed, local retaining walls and a few support piers, with colliders on the piers. The adjacent terrain mesh is cut only where it intrudes into a 3.2 m-wide rail corridor; high sections keep the ground below. The retained edges follow the scene DTM. The support layout and retaining walls are design estimates, not historic engineering drawings.

Road surfaces are not modified. The targeted tests sample the full corridor and pier footprints plus a buffer using the nearest road surface query, verify the bridge-to-approach coordinate/height match, and check that sampled buried track is covered by a cut. This is not a complete continuous clearance, traffic, building, or player-ground-height validation. The terrain mesh is clipped but general ground-height queries still use the underlying DTM: this is not a walkable station or playable rail surface. `tests/visual/peak-tram-kennedy-approach.html` shows an isolated WebGL scene with a terrain cut; full game WebGPU, lighting, and actual driving around the site remain unverified. The rest of the line and stations are unfinished.
