# Kennedy to MacDonnell Road: track extension

The unified scene includes approximately 420 m of experimental-profile track from the uphill end of the Kennedy Road bridge, past MacDonnell Road, the former Bowen Road stop, Brewin Path and May Road, to the lower passing-loop switch node (s≈544.8 m). See `docs/peak-tram-may-road-approach.md`. This is a track extension, **not a completed station** or surveyed alignment. It has rails, sleepers, a track bed, local retaining walls and support piers, with colliders on the piers. Terrain mesh is clipped only where it intrudes into the 3.2 m-wide rail corridor; the roadside retaining-wall top is capped below the queried road surface. The former flat soffit has been replaced in the road-covered core by a scene-fit masonry barrel vault, jambs and portal stones; see `docs/peak-tram-macdonnell-bridge.md` for its substantial geometric and verification limitations.

The additional section from MacDonnell to the former Bowen stop is about 52 m, with track, a local terrain cut and retaining faces; the next approximately 40 m passes under Magazine Gap Road in a schematic masonry vault. See `docs/peak-tram-magazine-gap.md`. Road samples across its ±3.5 m corridor did not find road surfaces, and the nearby street-light checks were extended to it. This does not establish clearance from buildings, trees or utilities. The road surface and traffic paths are not modified. Sampled track-to-road height differences across offsets up to 3.5 m include MacDonnell Road ribbons 418/8998 (minimum about 6.25 m) and an unnamed road ribbon 49091 (minimum about 5.96 m); these are scene indices, not OSM identifiers. Tests also sample the interpolated retaining-wall tops, pier footprints, terrain cuts, and street lamps. Nearest-road queries and discrete samples do not prove continuous vehicle clearance or structural integrity. They do not verify surrounding buildings, vegetation, utilities, station access, or vehicle and pedestrian movement.

The terrain mesh is clipped but general ground-height queries still use the underlying DTM: this is not a walkable station or playable rail surface. The OSM stop node sits around y=106 m in this trial profile, whereas published station elevation is around 95 m; neither should be treated as surveyed engineering data. The station's west-side platform, shelter, historical arch and safe access remain to be designed after resolving the mismatch. `tests/visual/peak-tram-kennedy-approach.html` is an isolated WebGL scene, not a full-game validation of this extension. The remainder of the line, stations and operating tram are unfinished.
