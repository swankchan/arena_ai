# Hong Kong Coliseum (紅館)

The arena is a photo-directed exterior interpretation, not a measured reconstruction. Its approximate centre (22.30128, 114.18196) is based on [Mapcarta's OSM-derived venue location](https://mapcarta.com/W102256410). [OSM way 102256410](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/102256410), version 18, denotes the larger stadium precinct, **not** the precise arena footprint. The building width, slope, height, podium and roof details are estimates; the DTM is not a surveyed podium elevation.

References consulted:

- [LCSD official historical and exterior photographs](https://www.lcsd.gov.hk/en/hkc/aboutus/photogallery/historicalphotos.html): inverted sloping light-coloured shell, dark roof perimeter, elevated concourse and support columns. The building is not red despite the local nickname.
- [Historical engineering description](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/coliseum-hong-kong/249516033): inverted-pyramid structure, corner supports, space-frame roof and precast concrete roof panels.
- [Venue guide](https://www.fantopia.io/shows/venue/hong-kong-coliseum-venue-guide.html): opened 27 April 1983; up to 12,500 seats depending on configuration.

The model has individual subtly varied facade panels, a dark ribbed roof perimeter, elevated platform, recessed dark concourse and columns. No interior or connected Hung Hom station is represented. The platform is not claimed to be a walkable reconstruction. Road topology and ground terrain are not altered.
