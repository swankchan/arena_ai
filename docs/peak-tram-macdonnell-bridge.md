# MacDonnell Road bridge over the Peak Tram

The active scene replaces the former small flat soffit with an estimated masonry barrel vault, stone jambs and individual portal arch stones under the existing MacDonnell Road road ribbon. The unchanged road geometry and AI paths remain on top. The [Gwulo bridge photo index](https://gwulo.com/node/42813/photos) includes a 1989 view, and [Checkerboard Hill](https://www.checkerboardhill.com/2011/02/the-peak-tram-trainspotters-edition/) describes the stone-arch crossings. The model is **inspired by the documented masonry bridge, not a verified reconstruction of its exact original geometry or current appearance**. The skewed road ribbon and the trial track are not surveyed; the vault covers only the central approximately 6 m where the full opening lies beneath the road, and its portal edges and missing exterior/parapet details need further work. Ordinary terrain and road mesh remain outside that core.

The opening is 3.7 m wide with a depressed 0.8 m rise; each crown is set 0.5 m below the lowest sampled nearby road surface. The track corridor is locally widened to fit, and generic cut walls are replaced by the arch jambs. Automated tests sample the bridge road ribbon, the arch/road relationship and a 4.1 m rail-to-soffit envelope at offsets up to 1.35 m; these are discrete geometry checks, not a swept 33 m vehicle test, a structural safety check, or proof of road/rail continuity. Road, building, lighting, traffic and player behaviour have not been inspected in the full game. `tests/visual/peak-tram-macdonnell-bridge.html` shows an isolated forced-WebGL view without road meshes; the ground height query still uses the DTM, and some terrain-cut edges have visible gaps in that view.
