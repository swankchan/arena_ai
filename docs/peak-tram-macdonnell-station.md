# MacDonnell Road platform (provisional)

The active scene now contains a west-side stepped platform, yellow edge stripe, dark handrails, timber bench, green steel bracket and small canopy, bilingual nameboard, and TSL night-light strips. Design cues come from the 2021 photo credited in `references/hongkong/peak-tram/README.md`; dimensions and the post-2022 arrangement are not verified. The approximate 19.7 m platform is intentionally a visual first stage and is **shorter than the 33 m model car**. It is not yet a complete station: stairs/paths to the street, platform expansion, a fully verified bridge and its road approaches, passenger movement, stop request and boarding are missing.

The platform is provisionally placed immediately downhill of the OSM stop node, on the experimental track profile. Its current rail heights span roughly 96–106 m: a published 95 m station elevation might refer to a lower point on a sloping platform, but that is only a possible explanation, not a resolved survey discrepancy. The terrain mesh is locally cut and the west retaining face moved out to the platform edge. Ground-height queries still follow the original terrain, so **it is not a walkable platform**; no station collision or traffic behaviour is claimed. Road-surface samples across the platform footprint found no overlap; this is not a continuous proof and does not check adjacent buildings, trees, utilities or actual train swept clearance. `tests/visual/peak-tram-macdonnell-station.html` is an isolated forced-WebGL view with clipped terrain only; the complete game, road network, WebGPU and night appearance have not been visually verified.
