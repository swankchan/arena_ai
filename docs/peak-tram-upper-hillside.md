# Passing loop to upper hillside: provisional track

The active scene extends the track from the end of the previously built lower passing-loop section at s=645 m to OSM node 9628742368 at s≈1014.2 m. It includes rails, sleepers, bed, local DTM mesh cuts, retaining faces and support piers with colliders. Where the experimental profile is above the terrain, the ground is retained under the estimated supports; where it is buried, a local corridor is clipped from the terrain mesh.

Reference [Checkerboard Hill (2019)](https://www.checkerboardhill.com/2019/07/hong-kong-peak-tram-passing-loop-rails/) describes a four-rail passing section, followed uphill by three rails with a shared middle rail, and finally two rails. The OSM branch centre traces are not an engineering track plan and are not consistently 1.524 m apart. This model joins the existing experimental four-rail section, transitions the branch centres to 1.524 m separation, draws a shared middle rail, and returns to two rails near the upper curve. The transition chainages 675, 820 and 840 m are visual estimates, **not surveyed switch positions**. The switch hardware, cable pulleys, guard rails and fencing still need modelling. This is not a verified replica or operating track alignment.

Tests sample rail-to-terrain cuts and road queries, test support footprints and nearby street-light poles/arms, and check joining coordinates; an isolated forced-WebGL view is in `tests/visual/peak-tram-upper-line.html`. These tests do not establish continuous swept-car, road, building, vegetation or utility clearance. General ground queries still use the original DTM; track and retaining faces are not walkable surfaces. Full game, driving and WebGPU have not been verified. The provisional track now continues beyond s≈1014 m to the upper station; see `peak-tram-barker-station.md` and `peak-tram-summit-approach.md` for unfinished work.
