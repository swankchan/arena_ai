# 凌霄閣 Peak Tower

The active scene contains a photo-directed **exterior** of the current building: the 2006-era cantilevered wok silhouette, curved ribbed underside, segmented metal shell, inset horizontal glazing, four banded piers, glass retail elevations, louvres, entrance signage, hillside foundations and Sky Terrace railings. The site uses [OSM way 143716612 v11](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/143716612), © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL, projected to approximately 68.6 × 28.6 m. The curved silhouette and internal structure are approximations, not architectural plans. [The operator's history](https://www.thepeak.com.hk/en/heritage/history-of-the-peak-tower) describes the 396 m site, 428 m terrace, Farrell's 1997 building and 2005–06 revitalisation; the model uses a 396 m datum and 428 m deck. Photographs used for exterior direction: [Peak Tower exterior](https://victoriapeakhongkong.com/guides/the-peak-tower) and [Hong Kong Tourism Board](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/place-to-go/travel.guide-the-peak-tower.html).

The lower core is inset to avoid the mapped Old Peak Road overlapping the OSM outer footprint; terrain-following foundations descend to the existing DTM and no road or territory terrain height is changed. The local building walls have collision, and an isolated 0.5 m road sample checked 6,731 points with zero collider intersections at 4.8 m vehicle height. The shell also clears sampled road points vertically, but the sample test is not a full vehicle simulation or a surveyed clearance. The OSM upper tram stop and approach nodes are outside the inset wall colliders; this is **not** a built station or a railway connection.

The interior, shops, public entrance circulation, escalators/lifts, accessible terrace and actual Peak Tram are not modelled; a player cannot use the building as a complete public facility. The south entrance and details are photo-directed estimates, not independently mapped entrances. Isolated visual review used `forceWebGL` and a local DTM mesh; full-game rendering, WebGPU, walking and driving remain unverified. See `peak-tram-research.md` for road and railway ground conflicts.
