# 凌霄閣 Peak Tower

The active scene contains a photo-directed **exterior** of the current building: the 2006-era cantilevered wok silhouette, curved ribbed underside, segmented metal shell, inset horizontal glazing, four banded piers, glass retail elevations, louvres, entrance signage, hillside foundations and Sky Terrace railings. The site uses [OSM way 143716612 v11](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/143716612), © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL, projected to approximately 68.6 × 28.6 m. The curved silhouette and internal structure are approximations, not architectural plans. [The operator's history](https://www.thepeak.com.hk/en/heritage/history-of-the-peak-tower) describes the 396 m site, 428 m terrace, Farrell's 1997 building and 2005–06 revitalisation; the model uses a 396 m datum and 428 m deck. Photographs used for exterior direction: [Peak Tower exterior](https://victoriapeakhongkong.com/guides/the-peak-tower) and [Hong Kong Tourism Board](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/place-to-go/travel.guide-the-peak-tower.html).

The lower core is inset to avoid the mapped Old Peak Road overlapping the OSM outer footprint; terrain-following foundations descend to the existing DTM. The small upper-station area is locally cut out of the terrain mesh to fit the platforms and track; road heights are unchanged. The local building walls have collision, and an isolated 0.5 m road sample checked 6,731 points with zero collider intersections at 4.8 m vehicle height. The shell also clears sampled road points vertically, but the sample test is not a full vehicle simulation or a surveyed clearance. The mapped upper tram stop and approach now have an approximately 32 m static track stub, 1,524 mm gauge rails, two platforms and glass safety barriers. The horizontal placement follows [OSM relation 3264047](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/3264047) and its end nodes (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL); platform widths and vertical levels are approximate and not surveyed. The local station terrain cut has no sampled road intersections; the platform ground-height query is limited to pedestrians. This is **not** a complete or operational station, and the static track does not connect to an operational railway.

The interior, shops, public entrance circulation, escalators/lifts, accessible terrace and actual Peak Tram are not modelled; the station platforms are not connected to a usable interior concourse; a player cannot use the building as a complete public facility. The south entrance and details are photo-directed estimates, not independently mapped entrances. The earlier exterior-only isolated visual review used `forceWebGL` and a local DTM mesh; the new station platforms and terrain cut have not been visually reviewed or play-tested; full-game rendering, WebGPU, walking and driving remain unverified. See `peak-tram-research.md` for road and railway ground conflicts.

Reference for the platform layout and modern glass barriers: [Hong Kong Extras](https://www.hongkongextras.com/peak_tram.html) and [Peak Tram ride photographs](https://victoriapeakhongkong.com/guides/the-peak-tram-ride). Platform access, as-built elevation and the precise barrier positions need field or plan verification.
