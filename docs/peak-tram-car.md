# Sixth-generation Peak Tram visual asset

`createPeakTramCar()` builds a **static, standalone visual asset**. It is not installed in the active scene, not rideable, and has no opening doors, movement or collision. `tests/visual/peak-tram-car.html` (add `?night`) shows it in an isolated forced-WebGL scene, not the game. No Google assets or proprietary images are embedded.

The [IMechE Peak Tram technical visit report](https://nearyou.imeche.org/docs/default-source/Hong-Kong/technical-visit--peak-tramways.pdf?sfvrsn=0) describes the then-planned 33 m sixth-generation cars and eight roll-on doors; the operator's [2022 announcement](https://www.thepeak.com.hk/sites/peak/files/2022-03/Peak%20Tram_6th%20Generation%20Tramcar%20Unveiling_Press%20Releaes_Eng_Final.pdf) describes the green body, panoramic windows and greater accessibility. The [2022 Commons image](../references/hongkong/peak-tram/README.md) and [2023 upper-terminus photograph](https://www.hongkongextras.com/peak_tram.html) inform the green lower panels, light roof, roof glazing and circular lights. Width 2.7 m, height, windows, interior, seating and mechanisms are visual estimates, not measured specifications.

Important integration blocker: the existing upper-station stub is only about 32.48 m long and the distance between its barrier centre lines is 2.56 m, less than the car's approximate 2.7 m body width before door trim. Consequently this asset is deliberately not placed there. The station, its guard doors, track and terrain must be reconsidered together, then the true car envelope, clearance and passenger/road collision must be tested. Neither the stand-alone rendering nor a test of model dimensions establishes real-world clearances. The force-WebGL day/night preview has been viewed; complete game WebGPU and actual night appearance are unverified.
