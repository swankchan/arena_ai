# Camera drone reference

The player aircraft is a **Mavic 3-inspired interpretation**, not a licensed DJI asset or an exact CAD replica. The reference photographs were inspected for the grey folding fuselage, compact legs, vent, black two-lens front gimbal, front obstacle cameras, four motors, tapered two-blade propellers and orange blade tips.

- [DJI Mavic 3 official support specifications](https://www.dji.com/support/product/mavic-3): unfolded without propellers 347.5 × 283 × 107.7 mm (length × width × height), 380.1 mm diagonal wheelbase; 3-axis gimbal and omnidirectional sensing. The source also lists a maximum 21 m/s horizontal speed (sport mode).
- [DJI official Mavic 3 low-noise propellers](https://store.dji.com/product/dji-mavic-3-low-noise-propellers): 23.9 cm diameter, two folding blades.
- [TechRadar Mavic 3 review and photographs](https://www.techradar.com/reviews/dji-mavic-3): top and front close-up visual references.

The geometry uses metre units, 0.380 m diagonal motor spacing and 0.239 m propeller diameter; shell and gimbal shapes and dimensions are approximations. Rotor pivots animate, but the camera gimbal is not independently stabilised. The existing 288 km/h flight mechanics have deliberately been retained for gameplay compatibility and are **not** a simulation of the Mavic 3 speed or aerodynamics.
