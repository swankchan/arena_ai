# Provisional Central Terminus platform and approach

The 2022 Central Terminus boarding point was moved uphill from the entrance beneath St John's Building. The OSM stop `n2525250101` and first bridge node `n2525250108` span s=0–32.69 m on the existing **unsurveyed** route/profile. The model uses that span for a pair of side platforms, rails, sleepers, yellow edges, side retaining faces and night wall-light strips, with a local terrain cut. Rail top is 0.28 m above the bed; platform tops are 0.57 m above the rail. The next static track section continues to the existing Kennedy Road bridge; some estimated piers support the elevated parts and narrow cuts clear the buried parts. Platform length, widths, heights, wall and support design are scene-fit estimates, **not measured construction plans**. The calculated profile rises about 10.4 m over the platform; neither this gradient nor level boarding with the 33 m tram has been verified.

The Cotton Tree Drive road ribbon is left untouched above the cutting. At 0.5 m along-track and 0.25 m lateral intervals, road query plus 0.25 m buffer is at least 5.2 m above the rail within the estimated tram width; this is not a continuous swept-volume test or a structural bridge check. The platform ground query is restricted to person mode and near the trial platform level so it does not replace overhead vehicle road heights. Support-box road checks are also discrete. The actual vehicle, platform/train doors, enclosing station roof, elevated road deck/abutments, lifts, the passage to the street-level waiting area, and passenger simulation are not yet implemented. The two rendered rail sections join geometrically; the full train ride remains unverified.

`tests/visual/peak-tram-central-platform.html` is an isolated WebGL preview containing only simplified terrain, road 720, platform and approach. Small sky-coloured gaps remain at cutting junctions, particularly near the station end and the road edge. This preview is not a full scene or WebGPU check; dynamic road traffic, adjacent buildings, supports, terrain joins, trees, station accessibility, night lighting and overhead clearances must be checked before calling the station complete.
