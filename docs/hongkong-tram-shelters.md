# Hong Kong tram stop shelters

Hong Kong Tramways' stop list supplies names, codes and positions along the route, not surveyed shelter footprints. [Hong Kong Extras](https://www.hongkongextras.com/trams.html) shows the narrow island and roadside shelters and bilingual stop signs; its photographs informed the simple roof, frame, glass windbreak, tactile platform edge and station-name boards. Existing termini retain their separately built shelters.

The renderer adds 109 ordinary shelter platforms, plus the eight existing terminus platforms, covering the 123 route assignments after deduplicating shared stop records. Each placement is an approximation: it searches around the stop chainage for clearance from all mapped track lines and sampled game road surfaces. Exact platforms, access routes and shelters are **not surveyed**. In particular, stops 110 and 50W required offsets of 25 m and 40 m respectively to avoid the current simplified game carriageways, so their placement and pedestrian accessibility need manual correction against reliable site data. No claim is made that these are the actual platform locations. Sampled roadway clearance does not prove absence of all conflicts with buildings, vehicle routes, street furniture, or traffic signals. WebGPU visual inspection is still required.
