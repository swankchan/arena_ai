# HK-B0038 華潤大廈 / China Resources Building

- [OSM way 107497701 v11](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/way/107497701/full.json), © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL: 49 levels, 178 m; the same height is reported by [CTBUH](https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/building/china-resources-building/2422), which counts 50 floors. The local OSM outline preserves the many closely spaced perimeter segments. Entrance nodes 6970776018, 8308187116, 8308187117, 8308187118 and 8308187131 determine ground-level openings; exact door widths are estimated.
- [Ronald Lu & Partners](https://rlp.asia/en/project/china-resources-building/) describes the later curtain-wall retrofit and rhythmic vertical features, landscaped courtyard and glazed-canopy passages. Photos were used for the blue-grey reflective glass and projecting silver fins. The glazing, 49 visual rows and spacing are approximations; no photographic assets are bundled.
- The old five-storey annex was demolished; the [architect's account](http://www.rlphk.com/eng/projects/sustainable-design/selected-projects/18/15/73/china-resources-building-hong-kong.html) describes removal of upper podium floors. This rendition covers the mapped **tower**, not St. Regis, the separate redeveloped podium, courtyard or its glazed pedestrian connections. Interior is not modelled.
- A road/collider grid sample and five entrance-node clearance checks pass; these are not a swept-vehicle test or surveyed door clearances. A standalone forceWebGL preview was inspected; full-game WebGPU was not verified.
