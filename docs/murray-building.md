# 美利大廈 / The Murray

The active Hong Kong scene includes a photo-directed **exterior**, located using [OSM way 85320844 v14](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/85320844), © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. The mapped oriented footprint is about 29.86 × 51.84 m. [Foster + Partners](https://www.fosterandpartners.com/projects/the-murray) describes the 1969 government building's recessed windows, multi-storey arches and former vehicle ramp, and its conversion to a hotel; their [project data](https://www10.aeccafe.com/blogs/arch-showcase/2018/07/06/the-murray-hotel-in-hong-kong-china-by-foster-partners/) cites 51.5 × 31.5 m and 93.83 m height. Floor-count conventions differ across sources. The simulated construction datum is the maximum mapped footprint-edge terrain sample (about 28.15 m); it is not a surveyed floor elevation. The tower height relative to this datum is approximately 89 m, to account approximately for the lower terrain at the downhill end.

The model has 966 individually recessed, skewed glazed bays with diagonal concrete sunshades, 22 genuinely open arcade arches with curved soffits and individual piers, an inset glazed lobby, bronze trim, indicative arrival-ramp railing, roof edge and terrain-following arcade paving. Bay, arch and entrance dimensions, colours and ramp location are photo-directed approximations, not construction drawings. The lobby is closed and the ramp is **not** a functional pedestrian route. The surrounding landscaped arrival court, public paths, actual former car-ramp layout, Old and Valuable Cassia tree, hotel interior and roof restaurant are not reproduced. The present model must not be described as a complete hotel or a surveyed replica.

The footprint's DTM edge heights vary roughly 21.9–28.2 m. Piers extend down to the existing terrain and paving samples the same terrain; no global pad or road-height modification was made. The isolated 0.5 m road/vehicle-height collision test checks 20,359 road points with zero hits, and sampled arcade centres have no pier collider. Isolated preview was rendered with `forceWebGL`; full-game WebGPU, walking, driving and the as-built landscaping remain unverified.
