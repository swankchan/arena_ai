# Hong Kong junction courtesy

Traffic already has same-link car-following and contact resolution. In addition, nearby cars on different links now project their current headings to detect a potential crossing within 35 m; one yields using arrival time, whether a vehicle is already in the junction, and a stable tie-breaker. The yielding car receives a braking cap and a stop-distance limit. This is a conservative local heuristic, not traffic-signal phase inference, a full future-turn reservation, lane-merge planning, or a guarantee that vehicles never collide. Cars on levels more than 3 m apart are excluded. Unrelated player-traffic and traffic-traffic collisions now compare relative heights, rather than disabling player collisions everywhere above the fixed altitude 2.6 m.

The heuristic has pairwise cost among the nearby traffic fleet and can over-yield when projected headings do not match actual turns. Initial staging, restaging and close merges can still overlap. Unit tests cover right-angle approaches, stable ordering, vehicles already in a junction, separated road levels, and player contact on elevated roads. Full-world congestion, FPS, yielding fairness, signal interaction and actual driving have not been validated.
