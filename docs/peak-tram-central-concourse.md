# Central Terminus: inferred concourse and moving-walkway connection

[Arup's project account](https://www.arup.com/en-us/projects/peak-tram-upgrade-project/) describes approximately 40 m of inclined moving walkways in a glazed, aluminium-clad spiral portal, a larger lower-ground waiting area, and a steep-gradient platform. The photographs inform the muted grey upper panels, warm timber-like lower panels, glass balustrades and linear lighting. The **plan, elevations, roof shape and access arrangements are not surveyed** and are scene-fit estimates, not a reconstruction of the real plan.

The existing street bay and trial platform are about 99 m apart in the scene. This model uses a 2.8 m simulated lift descent at the bay, a narrow lower hall and a 40 m rising walkway section. The hallway narrows near the platform to fit the existing platform, so it is **not a representation of the real 1,000+ person hall**. The line ends at a newly opened portal in the platform's end wall. It has warm day/night strips and local point lights. It is deliberately under the modeled roads, which are not moved. A small local terrain opening is provided around the entrance shaft and where the roof intersects ground; this is an approximation, not verified underground construction.

The inferred lift automatically calls/returns and carries a person who waits inside. Two estimated opposing travelator lanes move a person standing on them; tread geometry itself is static. The lift location is inferred to reconcile the model with existing road levels and does not claim the real lift is at this location. The upper and lower safety covers and automatic motion are gameplay simplifications, not actual doors or an accessibility-compliant installation. Person-mode ground queries select the lower floor only when the reference height is close; vehicle road selection remains unchanged. The platform, train-door alignment, glazed screen gates, station-scale waiting capacity, skylights and enclosing roof are not complete.

Tests sample the trial floor, road surface, roof/side-wall geometry and wall collision boxes, test a local centreline against the station entrance/tower/lift, raycast the station-end portal and shaft opening, and exercise lift and walkway kinematics. With a 0.35 m road query buffer and 0.4 m samples, the selected floor had over 4.25 m to sampled road height; this is not the underside of a measured deck, a continuous collision proof, a structural survey or accessibility certification. `tests/visual/peak-tram-central-concourse.html` is an isolated WebGL preview, including `?night` and `?portal`; complete-game WebGPU, player traversal, lift interaction, road traffic, adjacent buildings and all terrain seams remain unverified. The cut/junction gaps and full platform cover still need work.
