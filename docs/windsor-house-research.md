# 皇室堡 / Windsor House, Causeway Bay

- [OSM way 171882809 v6](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/way/171882809/full.json) is the five-storey retail podium, [way 149401356 v8](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/way/149401356/full.json) is Chubb Tower with OSM height 140 m and 42 levels. Both rings are preserved independently. [CTBUH](https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/building/windsor-house/10494) gives 142 m including functional-technical equipment, while commercial sources use other floor counts; the model uses the OSM 140 m and does not claim a measured roof elevation. Its 26 m podium and facade bay divisions are approximate.
- Street photos ([2025 tower and podium](https://propwiser.com.hk/en/Building/causeway-bay/windsor-house---chubb-tower/235), [entrance](https://www.valueproperties.com.hk/en-US/buildings/windsor-house--chubb-tower)) inform the burgundy crown, horizontal pale tower bands, dark glazing, vertical piers, multi-level corner shopfronts and retail displays. Display graphics are original approximations, not photographs of actual advertisements. The interior is not reproduced.
- The map contains [way 171882813 v4](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/way/171882813/full.json), a two-way service driveway ending at the north-west edge of the podium; the generated 8.4 m ribbon grazes the footprint. A short ground-level opening is used at the mapped corner, not a surveyed portal design. Existing roads are retained; sampled nearby road points are clear of authored facade colliders to 4.8 m. This is not an exact intersection proof or a driving test.
- Standalone forceWebGL inspection and targeted tests do not prove visual correctness in the complete WebGPU game.
