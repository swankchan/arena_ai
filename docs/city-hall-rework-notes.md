# City Hall rework — work in progress

Reference: [Antiquities and Monuments Office heritage appraisal](https://www.amo.gov.hk/filemanager/amo/common/form/dminfo/DM132_Related_Informatioin_En.pdf) and [official photographs](https://www.amo.gov.hk/en/historic-buildings/monuments/hong-kong-island/monuments_132/index.html). The appraisal identifies a 12-storey high block with north/south concrete window grids and blank east/west end walls; the L-shaped low block has granite walls, a north window band and west lozenge windows. It specifies a 20-foot diameter 12-sided shrine and a pond added around it in 2002. The [City Hall photo gallery](https://www.cityhall.gov.hk/en/hkch/aboutus/photogallery/asketchofthecityhall.html) documents the spiral staircase and sculpture.

The previous facade helper's positive offset points into the source polygons, hiding glass, entrance details and diamond windows in the solid shell. The City Hall frames now face outwards; a raycast regression checks the glass is in front of the wall. The shrine and its collision footprint now use the documented 6.096 m diameter; the reflecting pool and rectangular lawn are photo-directed approximations. An isolated Three.js WebGL render and a CPU depth-buffer rendering of the meshes were reviewed against the AMO photograph. These are not the game's WebGPU render and do not validate in-game lighting, terrain or all elevations.

The stair has now been modelled from the [Wikimedia Commons photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:HK_%E4%B8%AD%E5%8D%80_Central_%E6%84%9B%E4%B8%81%E5%A0%A1%E5%BB%A3%E5%A0%B4_Edinburgh_Place_City_Hall_Lower_Block_spiral_stairs_October_2024_R12S_02.jpg), with approximate location and dimensions; the upper slab is visual, not a walkable navigation surface. The garden has approximate trees and the two-section covered link now follows the garden edge rather than cutting across it. Three approximate perimeter runs, based on photographs rather than surveyed plans, have also been added. The current walkway is a simplified connection, not a verified reconstruction; dimensions and positions of non-OSM features are estimated. The original building shells are now cut at the same entrance gaps as the colliders; the glazed doors are still simplified opaque representations rather than modelled interiors. The OSM height tags are not independent surveyed architectural heights. Road-priority code has not yet been changed to the requested building-priority approach. A full in-game WebGPU view is still needed, and the simplified landscape, paths and interior should not be presented as a survey-accurate reconstruction.
