# World-Wide House research and model

World-Wide House (環球大廈), 19 Des Voeux Road Central, is the commercial property in Central, not the residential Universal Building. [Wong & Ouyang's project description](https://www.wongouyang.com/projects/worldwide-house/) identifies an important architectural feature: the office floor plate comprises **two overlapping squares**, expressed as two interlocking towers of different heights. The tower's disposition and columns were constrained by the MTR concourse beneath it. Its description also identifies ground-level MTR entrances. A generic single rectangular prism would miss this defining form.

[OSM way 295407372 v9](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/295407372) is tagged as a four-storey podium, with two mapped entrances at nodes 2991175226 and 2991175248. It is not a mapped office-tower outline. The mapped site is approximately 2,348 m² in the project's frame. The nearby covered pedestrian bridge is [OSM way 42293821](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/42293821); its vertical connections and destination still need checking before modelling.

[JLL](https://property.jll.com.hk/en/office-lease/hong-kong/central/world-wide-house-hkg-p-000kq5) reports 27 storeys, a retail podium and approximately 17,000 sq ft typical office floor plates; [LeasingHub](https://www.leasinghub.com/building/world-wide-house/6) describes 23 office floors, 4 retail floors and 16,693 sq ft typical floor area. [Emporis](https://www.emporis.com/buildings/120961/world-wide-house-hong-kong-china) lists 108 m and 29 floors; storey conventions differ among sources and must not be combined uncritically. Floor-plate area is a gross marketing figure, not a surveyed exterior outline.

Photos on [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:World-Wide_House) and property listings show blue/dark reflective glazing in a tight dark grid, an especially pronounced dark horizontal band, a low glazed retail base, and the large blue sign/screen near the MTR frontage. Some image-search results labelled with the property show incompatible buildings; cross-check photo subjects against location and independent sources before using them.

The podium and tower are now modelled as described below. Rendering, ground query and AI traffic use the separate road-width correction described in `building-priority-roads.md`.

## Further floor-plan evidence (2026-11-07)

[28Hse](https://www.28hse.com/en/estate/office/detail/world-wide-house-29593) publishes a plan explicitly labelled **5–11/F**; [HK Podium](https://hkpodium.com/building/13705/) publishes a visually matching plan labelled **Connaught Road Central** on one side and **Des Voeux Road Central** on the opposite side. Both show a stepped perimeter with opposite re-entrant corners, chamfered outer corners and lift/stair cores; the plan is consistent with the architect's overlapping-square description. This is materially better evidence than a generic box, and the road labels constrain orientation. Neither image supplies a scale, georeferenced tower corners or exact elevations. It does not establish the tower outline above 11/F or precisely which volume reaches which height. The listed gross floor areas should not be treated as a measured outline. Map alignment and the upper setback still need checking against photos and the podium, rather than assuming the full OSM podium is the tower.

[LeasingHub](https://www.leasinghub.com/building/world-wide-house/6) identifies the podium as G–3/F and offices 4–27/F and reports Central MTR Exit B. [Propwiser](https://office.propwiser.com.hk/en/Building/central/%E7%92%B0%E7%90%83%E5%A4%A7%E5%BB%88/240) reports ground-level MTR exits A and B and connections to the wider footbridge system; it does not establish the precise endpoints or heights of individual bridge segments. The covered OSM footway [42293821 v8](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/way/42293821/full.json) has four nodes and joins mapped podium entrance node 2991175226 at one end. Its other end, geometry at the destination and walking elevation remain to be verified before claiming a working connection.

## Implementation and limits

The podium follows OSM way 295407372. The office outline traces the HK Podium/28Hse 5–11/F plan; a 0.073 metres per image pixel scale and an offset were chosen to fit the OSM podium. This produces about 1,497 square metres, close to, but not independently verifying, published gross-area figures. The two-volume shape uses the traced stepped outline below 100 m and one square-like portion from 100 to 108 m. Which portion rises higher and its 100 m setback are photo-directed approximations, not established by the floor plan. The 108 m height is the Emporis estimate. Facade glazing, dark service band, retail base, two mapped vertex entrances and blue streetfront screen are interpretations based on photographs; dimensions, material appearance and signage are not survey data. Mapped entrances have collision gaps, not an interior or functioning underground MTR. The documented footbridge has **not** been modelled or made walkable because its destination and elevation have not been verified.

Two isolated views were rendered using Three.js WebGPU renderer's WebGL backend; they are not the game's complete scene or a WebGPU verification. Road clearance was previously checked in `building-priority-roads.md`; the resulting minimum margin is small and in-game vehicle and tram clearance remains unverified.

The DTM sampled within the podium varies approximately 4.59–5.23 m in game coordinates, while the model's centre datum is 4.81 m. Street-level storefront bottoms and colliders follow the local terrain rather than ending at the centre datum; entrance gaps are kept open. This fixes floating or buried storefront edges without flattening the site or changing adjacent roads. It does not establish surveyed street levels, level an interior, or diagnose uneven ground elsewhere in Hong Kong.
