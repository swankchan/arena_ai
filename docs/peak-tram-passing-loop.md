# Lower passing-loop track (partial)

The active scene now contains the lower approximately 100 m of the OSM route's two-branch section, from the lower switch node at s≈544.8 m to s=645 m on each branch. The two branch paths, 1.524 m rails, sleepers and a shared bed are installed. A single terrain cut spans both tracks and the median, with estimated outside retaining faces. The paths meet at the lower node but the switch rails, guides, pulleys, cables, protective fences and upper connection are **not implemented**. Both branches terminate abruptly at s=645; this is not yet a complete passing loop or a usable railway. The approximate track profile is not surveyed.

The 2019 [passing-loop photos](https://www.checkerboardhill.com/2019/07/hong-kong-peak-tram-passing-loop-rails/) show multiple rails, sleepers and cable hardware; the present geometry is preliminary, not a complete reconstruction. Discrete tests check that the two track centres, their 1.35 m edges and median are within the terrain cut, sample nearest road surfaces, check separation in the central part, and audit source street lamps in the local bounding box. No source lamps were found in the broader scanned corridor. These tests do not certify track switches, dynamic passing clearance, adjacent buildings, vegetation, utilities or railway operations. General ground-height queries remain based on DTM, not the cut bed; the cut can reach over 7 m below DTM and may have visual edge gaps.

`tests/visual/peak-tram-passing-loop.html` was inspected in an isolated forced-WebGL view; the rest of the game, the working trams, terrain collision and WebGPU were not checked. The rest of the branch pair and the connection to Barker Road and the upper station remain unbuilt.
