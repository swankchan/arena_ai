# Peak Tram Central street entrance: provisional scene fit

The entrance model is separate from the office tower and the relocated boarding platform. The street reference photographs and credits are in `references/hongkong/peak-tram/central-terminus/`; see `peak-tram-central-terminus-research.md` for source details and the unresolved road/track/terrain levels. This placement uses an estimated location at the downhill, short end of the OSM St John's footprint, not surveyed entrance or artwork coordinates. It is subject to correction against an accurate site plan.

The model provides a grey stone entrance with an actual opening, red reveals, sign, red paving, a small tentative waiting space with queue rails and warm night strips, and an elliptical pool and black-patinated bronze-like 10 m *Eye of Infinity*. The torus is a simplified approximation; 440 individually placed illuminated specks represent the real artwork's numerous tiny perforations, but the model is **not physically perforated**. The artwork's lighting does not yet respond to tram arrival. The earlier tower's lobby divider has been shortened so it does not block the estimated entrance. Ground-hugging paving does not alter roads. The raised pool is an approximation to avoid burying it in the coarse sloping DTM; precise edges, planting, contours, accessibility and drainage need further work.

`tests/peak-tram-central-entrance.test.cjs` samples paved mesh vertices, collider boxes and road query with 0.2 m buffer, and checks a clear entrance centre; it is not a continuous vehicle collision proof. `tests/visual/peak-tram-central-entrance.html` and its `?night` variant are isolated WebGL previews with only simplified terrain and one road, not the full world or WebGPU. The hall is **not connected to the provisional new platform**, and the tram is not operational. Scene position, adjoining street furniture, trees, full roads, player access and night appearance remain to be verified in the complete game.

The small initial waiting space now has a ceiling and a tentative side ticket counter. This is only the entrance bay, not a reconstruction of the full 1,300-person concourse. The desk and ceiling layout are inferred, and the unobstructed route to the relocated platform remains unmodelled.
