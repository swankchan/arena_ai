# Upper approach and terminus joint

The approximate single-track approach from s=1116 m to the mapped terminus approach node (s≈1232.2 m) is installed with sleepers, bed, conditional terrain cuts, retaining faces and supports. Over its final 15 m it gradually moves approximately 7.4 cm laterally to meet the existing upper station track centre. Track-centre elevation and gauge are matched. The upper station track corridor is widened to 3.30 m between barrier centres for the estimated 2.7 m wide tram; barrier colliders retain a sampled pedestrian route to the concourse. These dimensions are scene-fitting estimates, not a measured station plan.

Sampled terrain, roads, supports, lamps and the terminal joint are checked in `tests/peak-tram-summit-approach*.test.cjs`; an isolated WebGL view is in `tests/visual/peak-tram-summit-approach.html`. This does not prove full vehicle swept clearance, real-world platform geometry, gameplay, WebGPU rendering or complete accessibility. The existing terminus track is only about 32.5 m long while the estimated vehicle is 33 m; stop location, buffer, doors and actual vehicle operation need further work. The stretch s=1070–1116 is now joined by a provisional station and road crossing; see `peak-tram-barker-station.md` for major unfinished work. The railway is not playable.
