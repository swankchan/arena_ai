# Barker Road approach (partial)

The provisional single track now continues from the upper loop's OSM node 9628742368 (s≈1014.2 m) to s=1070 m, before Barker Road station (OSM node 988059821, s≈1082.5 m). Rails, sleepers, bed, conditional DTM mesh cuts, retaining faces and piers are installed in the scene. A separate WebGL view and sampled terrain, road, light and seam checks accompany this section. These do not prove continuous clearance or functioning train operation; terrain height queries still use the uncut DTM.

The historic station, bridge and track from s=1070 onward are not yet built. The [Antiquities Advisory Board appraisal](https://www.aab.gov.hk/filemanager/aab/common/historicbuilding/en/N26_Appraisal_En.pdf) describes a 1919 station with a flat roof over the track and western platform, masonry buttresses, arched openings with keystones, ornamental ironwork, annex and brick walkway. The road passes under the uphill end. A preliminary sampled query found Plantation Road (road index 36109) about 4–13 m below the trial track across the broad corridor near s≈1099–1115: a bridge/road arrangement needs a proper clearance audit, rather than laying plain track and piers across it. Dimensions and placement remain to be researched; profile heights are not survey data.

The upper terminus connection also remains unresolved: the existing station's internal barriers are approximately 2.56 m apart whereas the estimated tram car is 2.7 m wide. This is not a playable end-to-end route.
