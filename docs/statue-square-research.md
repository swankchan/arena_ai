# Statue Square: sources and reconstruction limits

The square is split by Chater Road. OpenStreetMap relation [6970394](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/6970394) has two separate outer rings: south garden [way 42293434](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/42293434) (v15), and north garden [way 25607730](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/25607730) (v9). Both geometries are already in `tools/data/hongkong/parks.json`; they must not be joined across the road.

The Hong Kong Film Services Office [location photographs](https://fpf.ccidahk.gov.hk/en/location/detail.php?id=1369) show the paved garden, trees, water features and angular white shelters. The [Antiquities and Monuments Office](https://www.amo.gov.hk/en/heritage-trails/cw-trails/central/section-a/central-a2/index.html) identifies the remaining statue as Sir Thomas Jackson, not Queen Victoria (whose statue is now in Victoria Park). Photographs and the [historical description](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statue_Square) indicate that Jackson faces the Court of Final Appeal.

## Mapped features to use, not invented placements

- [Node 9705188417](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/9705188417), v11: Jackson statue at 22.2810055, 114.1596580. The node establishes its mapped location, not the detailed geometry of the person or plinth.
- [Way 295407496](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/295407496), v2: southern water feature. [Way 295407519](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/295407519), v2, and [way 1242565441](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1242565441), v1, describe adjoining water features in the centre. [Way 295407520](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/295407520), v2, is a northern decorative fountain. These are OSM geometry rather than a verified engineering survey; photos are needed to distinguish levels, surfaces and artwork.
- Mapped 4.5 m high white concrete gabled weather-shelter roofs: [1242565444](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1242565444), [1242565445](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1242565445), [1242565448](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1242565448) and [1242565450](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1242565450), all v1. Consult photographs for the rhythm of the folded roof and supports; the OSM outlines alone do not describe structural details.
- The northern garden has mapped fountain [way 107324771](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/107324771), v2.
- The Cenotaph is a **separate nearby site east of the north garden**, not inside its mapped ring. [Way 737551130](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/737551130), v4, tags it limestone, 10.66 m high, centred approximately at 22.281632, 114.160575; surrounding stepped areas are separately mapped. Use the footprint and historical photographs with care: the exact outline of each monument tier is not established by a single polygon. Avoid putting it in the middle of the north garden. See also the [AMO heritage trail](https://www.amo.gov.hk/en/heritage-trails/cw-trails/central/section-a/central-a2/index.html).

## Implementation and limits

`statue-square-architecture.js` retains the two separate OSM outer rings and the mapped water, shelter, statue and monument coordinates. `statue-square-world.js` uses a terrain-following, road-cleared paving grid, four folded canopies, five mapped water polygons, two photo-inspired artworks, trees, planting and an east-facing Jackson figure. The Cenotaph is separately placed on the mapped monument footprint. The pavilion folds and columns, fountain reliefs and jets, Jackson likeness and plinth, tree/planter layout, benches, monument tiers and ornaments are **approximations**, not measured details; the figure is procedural rather than a scan or faithful sculptural portrait. The pools are represented as water surfaces with low edging, not excavated basins. Features have simplified colliders; the path surfaces do not add walkable raised levels.

The mapped pool and memorial interiors were sampled against the road query in a targeted test, and paving and trees are checked against that query. This does not prove road, traffic, accessibility, tree or building clearance for the whole scene. No road geometry or traffic paths were changed as part of this work. The isolated scene was visually checked with Three.js WebGL fallback; it was **not** a full-game WebGPU validation. The Cenotaph should be reviewed together with City Hall and the surrounding road and footways in the actual game.
