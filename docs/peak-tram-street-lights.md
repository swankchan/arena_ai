# Street-light adjustment at the Kennedy section

The existing Hong Kong street-light source has two poles intersecting the previously built Kennedy bridge/approach and its prospective tram envelope (source indices 4642 and 4643). The runtime now places both at nearby estimated positions away from that section, preserving their arms and lights; source CSV and generated data are unchanged. The correction is keyed to the complete original record and fails closed if that source record or a prior clearance override changes. Placement is an in-game compromise, not a surveyed relocation. A third source light (373) is moved near Brewin Path because its earlier street-clearance override placed it on the extended rail corridor; its original override is checked and its new base height is recalculated. See `docs/peak-tram-brewin-path.md`.

`tests/peak-tram-street-light-clearance.test.cjs` checks adjusted positions against the currently built section, its estimated vertical car envelope and all nearby source light poles/arms, and samples road queries at the new pole positions. It does not certify building, tree, utility-wire, actual road geometry or continuous vehicle clearances, and no full-game day/night visual check has been performed. This audit does not cover unbuilt parts of the railway; they must be checked when added. The supplied dataset contains street lamps; it is not evidence that all utility poles or overhead cables have been mapped.
