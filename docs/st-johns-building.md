# 聖約翰大廈 St John's Building

The office tower above the entrance to the Peak Tram Central Terminus is based on [OSM way 179091496](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/179091496), v4, © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. Its 31.2 × 13.3 m oriented bounding rectangle is used as the photo-directed upper volume, not a measured building survey. The 1983 building has 21 office floors according to its [owner](https://www.hshgroup.com/our-businesses/commercial-properties/st-johns-building), while a [LegCo project document](https://www.legco.gov.hk/yr17-18/chinese/panels/edev/papers/edev20180625cb4-1292-1-ec.pdf) counts 22 storeys including ground. The 78.45 m height, 3.15 m pitch, canopy and column positions are approximations, not published architectural dimensions.

The 2009 photo and source/attribution are stored in `references/hongkong/st-johns-building/`. The mass has rounded corners, grey/silver cladding, fine horizontal and vertical seams, 714 dark rounded rectangular windows, 21 repeated office courses, a solid crown and parapet, recessed lobby and chrome columns. A subset of windows and soffit strips have time-dependent warm node-material emission. Preview is `tests/visual/st-johns-building.html` (add `?night`); it is isolated WebGL with a simplified local terrain and road ribbon, not full-game WebGPU verification.

The upper tower and column collision boxes are checked against sampled full-road surfaces with a 0.25 m buffer and 4.5 m road vehicle envelope; the central pilotis deliberately has no solid collider. No road or terrain was altered for the tower. This discrete check does not establish continuous clearance, building foundations, full-scene terrain fit or dynamic driving. The 2022 Peak Tram entrance, Eye of Infinity, concourse and relocated platform **are not built** by this tower model; they require the separate multi-level integration described in `peak-tram-central-terminus-research.md`. The lobby screen is a provisional visual backdrop, not a walkable or validated station connection.
