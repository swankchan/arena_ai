# Barker Road crossing abutments

The two pairs of placeholder pin-like supports at chainages 1097 and 1116 m are replaced with estimated full-width, coursed-stone abutments beneath the deck. They follow the uncut DTM at the feet; sampled road footprints and a 4.5 m vehicle-height envelope are checked against their colliders. Neither dimensions nor masonry are surveyed. The opening infill and retaining-face work is described in `peak-tram-barker-earthworks.md`.

This is a scene-fit draft: the full actual skew bridge, its connections to adjacent retaining walls, vehicle dynamics, road/track clearance and exact landscape remain unverified. The isolated WebGL view includes one generated road ribbon, not the complete game environment.
