# Peak Tram Kennedy Road bridge

An initial steel bridge and its short rail segment are now added to the unified Hong Kong scene. The geometry follows the tentative joint Peak Tram profile; the estimated bridge deck extends slightly beyond the OSM bridge way so four support posts can sit outside the road. Rails, sleepers, dark deck, side walkways and triangular trusses are inspired by [Owennson's 2021 photo](../references/hongkong/peak-tram/README.md), taken before the 2022 rebuild. Support dimensions, locations and profile are inferred rather than surveyed; current appearance and exact ground conditions remain to be checked. There is no tram operation or connection to the full railway yet.

No road or terrain geometry is altered here. `tests/peak-tram-kennedy-bridge.test.cjs` samples the scene road query under the bridge and around its supports. At its sampling resolution the bridge underside has over 5.2 m clearance over Kennedy Road, and support pads do not intersect the queried roads with a 0.5 m buffer. This does not cover all road layers, all actual meshes, neighbouring buildings, moving cars, continuous swept volumes or construction tolerances; it does not certify road smoothness or real-world elevations. The independently modelled bridge was viewed in an isolated WebGL test; it has not been verified in the full game or WebGPU. Rails beyond this bridge and stations remain incomplete.
