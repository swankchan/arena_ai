# Central venues — research queue

This is a research queue, **not a completion list**. Hang Seng headquarters, Hong Kong Club Building, Prince’s Building and Mandarin Oriental have been modelled from this request; other entries remain outstanding. Venue sizes, placement, external materials, access and road conflicts must be checked per site before modelling. Avoid using a generic box as a finished landmark.

- **IFC Mall:** [PCP Architects](https://pcparch.com/work/ifc-hong-kong) describe the retail podium and roof garden; [HKTB](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/place-to-go/travel.guide-ifc-mall.html) gives 8 Finance Street. Existing One/Two IFC tower models are not the mall. Research its actual footprint, bridges, Apple store, entrances and road-underpass before adding it.
- **Hang Seng Bank Headquarters:** 83 Des Voeux Road Central; OSM way 28825127. [Architectural description](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hang_Seng_Bank_Headquarters_Building) records 137 m, 27 storeys and east/west windowless service cores; [facade description](https://www.emporis.com/buildings/120572/hang-seng-bank-building-hong-kong-china) describes silver aluminium and green reflective glass. Completed separately in source; details and ground entrance location are photo-directed approximations.
- **Prince's Building:** [10 Chater Road, completed 1965, 29 storeys with a six-level retail podium](https://www.corporatelocations.com.hk/office-for-rent/building/704/Princes-Building/); OSM tower way 107324768 and retail podium 42293788. Model uses [CTBUH’s 110 m estimate](https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/building/princes-building/19409), not a measured height, and [current facade photographs](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Prince%27s_Building_-_Exterior). The exterior is photo-directed and the entrance is approximate; connected bridges to nearby buildings are not yet modelled or position-verified.
- **Mandarin Oriental:** [official hotel press kit](https://press.mandarinoriental.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/2c322f8e69374303fc5a7e90153c9459.pdf), 5 Connaught Road Central, opened 1963, renovation in 2006. Photo-directed model uses current enclosed guestroom glazing, horizontal floor bands, rooftop fan/name and black granite entrance, not the historical balconies. [CTBUH estimates 92 m](https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/building/mandarin-oriental/19408); OSM way 42293822 is a hotel **landuse parcel**, not a surveyed building footprint. The modeled mass is an 80% inset approximation inside the parcel to leave mapped access roads open; entrance position and exterior dimensions are approximations, not a survey, and no interior is claimed.
- **World-Wide House:** [19 Des Voeux Road Central, 1980, 27 office floors](https://www.cbdofficehk.com/world-wide-house); [OSM way 42293733](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/42293733) identifies the correct commercial landuse parcel, but does not provide a surveyed tower footprint. [Emporis](https://www.emporis.com/buildings/120961/world-wide-house-hong-kong-china) records 108 m, 29 floors; other sources count 27. Check photo-directed tower mass and road boundary before modelling; do not confuse it with Universal Building at another site.
- **Hong Kong Club Building:** [CUHK architectural index](https://hkapi.lib.cuhk.edu.hk/items/138e61fa-326f-477d-acc0-0d89aa894f0b) credits Harry Seidler and records 1984, 22 storeys. [Photographs](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Hong_Kong_Club_Building) show horizontal projecting sunshades, dark glazing, curved crest, side piers and concave podium with arched entrance. OSM way 107324765 is tagged **landuse**, not a verified building footprint. Model complete as a photo-directed approximation inside that parcel; 87.4 m and 23 floors per [CTBUH](https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/building/the-hong-kong-club-building/13061); [the architect](https://seidler.net.au/earlier/projects/020Sub.html) describes the 34 m prestressed span and flamed granite. Curved crown, fins, recessed windows, entrance arch and podium garden are modelled; no surveyed architectural plan or club interior is claimed.
- **American Club:** [AIA Hong Kong](https://www.aiahk.org/locations/the-american-club-hong-kong-3/) and [Tanglin Club](https://www.tanglinclub.org.sg/reciprocal-clubs/reciprocal-clubs/118-china/hong-kong/237-the-american-club-hong-kong-town-club.html) identify Town Club on 48–49/F, Two Exchange Square, already modelled as an office tower. Country Club is a distinct location at 28 Tai Tam Road. Do not invent a second stand-alone Central building or claim the interior is present.
- **Entertainment Building:** [P&T neo-classical building and retail podium](https://www.greg-ling.com/ent-bdg), 30 Queen's Road Central, 1993. Photograph current street-facing podium, rotunda and crown; verify actual height and footprint.
- **Shun Tak Centre and Hong Kong–Macau Ferry Terminal:** [architectural history and exterior photographs](https://zolimacitymag.com/hong-kongs-skyline-icons-the-shun-tak-centre-gateway-to-macau/): two unequal glazed towers with red structural frames, porthole podium, enclosed link and waterfront ferry terminal. Need model ferry piers, two heliports, and road / footbridge clearances together, not just two towers.
- **Chater Garden:** [OSM way 148784038 v8](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/148784038), [photographic collection](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Chater_Garden). Contains mature trees, paths, pools and public plaza; map paths and water features without interfering with adjacent roads, Court of Final Appeal, HSBC or existing statue-square facilities.

### 娛樂行 / Entertainment Building — completed photo-directed model

* **30 Queen's Road Central**; Palmer & Turner, completed 1993. CTBUH gives 32 storeys and 186.5 m architectural height, with highest occupied floor at 146.9 m: https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/building/entertainment-building/2084 . Greg Ling describes the neoclassical building and modern retail entrance: https://www.greg-ling.com/ent-bdg . Exterior photographs: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Entertainment_Building and https://valueproperties.com.hk/en-US/buildings/entertainment-building .
* The authored base follows OSM building way [233135905](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/way/233135905/full.json), the 145 m office portion [233135920](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/way/233135920/full.json), 145–160 m setback [1284550012](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/way/1284550012/full.json), 160–165 m crown [1284550013](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/way/1284550013/full.json), and lantern [1284550018](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/way/1284550018/full.json). These are OSM mapper geometry rather than independently verified surveys; the roof between the outlines is an approximation of mapped sloping roof parts 1284550014–1284550017, and the dome is approximated from 1284550019. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
* Photo-directed details include the stone facade, dark inset glazing, diamond lattice, paired monumental columns, stepped pyramidal roof, lantern, and modern multi-material retail frontage. Advertisements and individual window layout are interpretive, not replicas. Mapped entry nodes include the main entry 9189030010; the ground-level collider has a 3.2 m passage centred there. Sampling of the podium and tower perimeter found no conflict with mapped game roads. WebGPU visual validation remains outstanding.

### 遮打花園 / Chater Garden — photo-directed landscape

* OSM garden [way 148784038](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/way/148784038/full.json), version 8, defines the site; © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Its projected area is approximately 11,326 m², not a claimed official measurement. DTM elevations are game terrain, not surveyed levels.
* References: [visitor photo of the pool, landscaping and palms](https://tripadvisor.com/LocationPhotoDirectLink-g294217-d5484289-i554321009-Chater_Garden-Hong_Kong.html), [LCSD Tree Walk sign photographed on site](https://tripadvisor.com/LocationPhotoDirectLink-g294217-d5484289-i96270058-Chater_Garden-Hong_Kong.html), [waterfall photograph](https://tripadvisor.com/LocationPhotoDirectLink-g294217-d5484289-i478766240-Chater_Garden-Hong_Kong.html), and [visitor description of the open gathering area and western Tree Walk](https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/Attraction_Review-g294217-d5484289-Reviews-Chater_Garden-Hong_Kong.html). Photos show green rails, paved tree walks, palms, trimmed shrubs and a dark two-tier spill feature in a blue pool.
* Paving, planting and pool locations are photo-directed scene approximations, not mapped or surveyed interior geometry. Ground is sampled from the DTM and skips game road surfaces; the pool is deliberately collidable. Trees share the existing major-park tree renderer. WebGPU visual validation remains outstanding.
