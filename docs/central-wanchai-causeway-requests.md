# Further requested buildings — research, not completion

## General Post Office (Connaught Place)

The 1976 General Post Office at 2 Connaught Place is requested despite its planned redevelopment. [Development Bureau](https://www.devb.gov.hk/en/legco_matters/replies_to_legco_questions/index_id_10336.html) says the existing building is to be demolished after reprovisioning; this project must represent the requested building, not a speculative replacement mall. [HKFP's 2026 photographic report](https://hongkongfp.com/2026/09/05/hkfp-lens-love-at-last-sight-bidding-farewell-to-hong-kongs-modernist-general-post-office-after-50-years/) describes a five-storey modernist building. [Wikimedia's exterior photos](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Exterior_of_General_Post_Office,_Hong_Kong) show long ribbon windows, deep vertically divided lower windows, the series of entrance canopies, signage and the forecourt. Site boundaries, collision with existing IFC and transport layers, interior and forecourt levels still need to be verified before modelling.

## Wan Chai Government Offices compound

The three adjacent buildings in the compound are Revenue Tower, Wanchai Tower and Immigration Tower. [The three-tower account](https://www.inmediahk.net/node/%E6%94%BF%E7%B6%93/%E7%81%A3%E4%BB%94%E5%8C%97%E4%B8%89%E6%94%BF%E5%BA%9C%E5%A4%A7%E6%A8%93%E6%93%AC2026%E5%B9%B4%E6%8B%86-%E8%AD%B0%E5%93%A1%E6%89%B9%E4%BB%8D%E6%96%A5%E8%B3%87%E4%BF%AE%E6%B5%AA%E8%B2%BB) identifies the three by these names; [this account](https://channelchk.com/%E5%8D%B3%E6%99%82%E7%86%B1%E8%A9%B1/%E5%85%A5%E5%A2%83%E8%99%95%E7%B8%BD%E9%83%A8%E5%91%8A%E5%88%A5%E7%81%A3%E4%BB%94-%E6%98%8E%E9%81%B7%E5%B0%87%E8%BB%8D%E6%BE%B3-%E5%85%A5%E5%A2%83%E8%99%95%E5%89%AF%E8%99%95%E9%95%B7-34%E5%80%8B%E5%AF%92%E6%9A%91%E6%9C%8D%E5%8B%99%E5%B8%82%E6%B0%91%E5%88%9D%E5%BF%83%E4%B8%8D%E8%AE%8A) notes that Immigration Tower also housed the Treasury. The user's phrase '前庫務大樓' may refer to Immigration Tower; this is not confirmed. Do not silently label it as an independent fourth building. `tools/data/hongkong/landmarks.json` has OSM ways 27087037 (Revenue Tower; tagged 180 m/50 levels), 27087041 (Wanchai Tower; tagged 137 m/33 levels), and 510722228 (Immigration Tower; tagged 180 m/50 levels). These tags are not a surveyed elevation or an assertion that the totals match every floor-numbering convention. [Wikimedia](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Wanchai_Tower) has facade photographs. Each building needs independent modelling and commit, including the surrounding transport and podium connections.

## SOGO / East Point Centre

The Causeway Bay store is at 555 Hennessy Road ([address and MTR connection](https://www.hongkong.net/shopping/sogo-hong-kong.html)). [OSM building relation 13274483](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/13274483) contains outline [way 294141091 v8](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/way/294141091/full.json), mapped four-level retail base [way 988042430 v5](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/way/988042430/full.json), upper 23-level [way 198586269 v6](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/way/198586269/full.json), and upper 15-level [way 220977125 v3](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/way/220977125/full.json); the parts' OSM minimum-level tags are explicitly marked `fixme` and do not constitute verified storey heights. Hennessy Road runs along the south side and Lockhart Road along the north. The building front has large advertising, a distinct blue SOGO sign, entrance canopy and shopfronts; [street photos](http://www.luxstate.com/hk-retail/mall/hong-kong/sogo) are a useful reference. The checked 1 m grid of the outline did not intersect `fullRoadSurfaceAt` center samples, but this is not a continuous clearance test or a tram/platform check. The height, physical entrances, advertisements, upper facade and interiors remain to be independently checked. A crude procedural draft was visually rejected and was **not** integrated.

### 郵政總局與道路衝突診斷及外觀

建築現存前原址以 OSM [主樓 way 16281866 v17](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/way/16281866/full.json)（五層，30.1 m）及[後翼 way 184767036 v5](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/way/184767036/full.json)（三層、石灰石，未標高度）記錄；[整體輪廓 way 1047782723 v8](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/way/1047782723/full.json)及[辦事處入口 node 7175416413](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/node/7175416413)作交叉核對。外觀參考 [Docomomo 的建築及功能描述](https://docomomo.hk/demolition-threat-hong-kongs-general-post-office/)、[外牆及歷史照片](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Exterior_of_General_Post_Office,_Hong_Kong)；主立面有細長深窗、水平實牆帶、入口弧形雨篷及中英文招牌，非純白方盒。

`node tools/cities/hongkong/check-general-post-office.mjs` 以 1 m 網格檢查現有道路和 IFC ring：主樓內未採到道路點；後翼內採到 48538（標有 tunnel flag）248 點、48539 9 點及 44961 7 點，當前查詢高度約 5.21–5.32 m，與翼樓中心 DTM 約 5.22 m 接近；兩部分均未採到 IFC overlap。這不是連續碰撞檢驗，不能斷定所有道路或相鄰建築無衝突。路網及高度不是工程實測；其後核對 [OSM way 1171889798 v1](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/way/1171889798/full.json)，該路標記為私家單程 service driveway、`tunnel=building_passage`，穿越後翼中軸；因此改為兩端開口、保留兩側長牆。後翼高度 15 m、開口淨高 7.5 m 均屬遊戲估算，非測量；私人出入口未做閘。旁邊 [way 768584593](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/way/768584593/full.json) 為私家雙線車道，遊戲推算路寬 8.4 m，與主樓後牆略擦碰，暫改為 7.6 m 並保留兩線；不是實測路寬。新模型採照片定形的窗、遮陽板、帶狀牆面和入口簷篷；輪廓來自 OSM，但立面尺寸與細節為估算。2,625 個附近道路網格點的 4.8 m 高度碰撞測試沒有命中；這不是連續性或駕駛驗證。室內及後翼細部未測量，未在完整 WebGPU 遊戲驗證。
