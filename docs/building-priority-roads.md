# Building-priority road correction: World-Wide House

The four-storey podium at World-Wide House is mapped as [OSM way 295407372 v9](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/295407372). The generated game road ways 17569 and 17570 are parts of Des Voeux Road Central; their 11.6 m width is an inferred rendering width, not an OSM kerb survey. The 17569 ribbon slightly overlaps the OSM podium footprint. Centreline clearance from that footprint is approximately 5.69 m, so this is a narrow edge conflict, **not** evidence that the road centreline passes through the building.

`building-priority-roads.js` supplies a shared gameplay/render override of 11.2 m for both adjoining ways. It preserves the source centrelines, endpoint topology, one-way direction and three lanes, and applies to road rendering, road queries, map, elevation, derived paint and the AI lane offset. The original generated data remains unchanged. This width is a pragmatic geometry correction based on a mapped outline, not an assertion of actual measured road width. The nearby tram centrelines are not shifted. Regenerating the tram network against the corrected road height changed 24 eastbound height samples by at most 0.01 m and changed nine overhead span records; the source track x/z coordinates are unchanged. A test intersects the rendered-style extended road ribbon with the OSM podium and samples the eastbound rails near the conflict; it also checks that the two AI graph links still match their respective roads and share the corrected lane width. These checks do not validate every road user, marking, tram paving, pedestrian crossing or real-world kerb in the full scene. Full-game visual and driving validation is still needed.

The OSM podium is not yet a completed World-Wide House building model; tower footprint, facade and elevated walkway need separate photo and source research. The Mandarin Oriental hotel polygon is a landuse area, not a mapped building footprint, so its current inset model cannot be expanded to the entire parcel on the assumption that the hotel occupies all of it. No wholesale road alteration has been made around it without better footprint evidence.
