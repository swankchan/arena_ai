# Brewin Path to May Road

The active trial track continues approximately 107 m from OSM node 26412146 to the May Road stop node, including the rail bed, sleepers, retaining faces and local terrain cuts. A scene-fit stone barrel vault sits under the fully road-covered core of the May Road crossing (road ribbons 36167/42840); road surfaces and vehicle paths are not changed. Historical sources describe May Road bridge and a west-side platform above it, including [Gwulo](https://gwulo.com/media/23602) and [Checkerboard Hill](https://www.checkerboardhill.com/2011/02/the-peak-tram-trainspotters-edition/). The vault is an estimate based on the scene's trial profile, not surveyed dimensions or a faithful finished bridge: only its central 6 m is modelled; skew, road approaches, full facade and parapet remain unfinished. **May Road station itself is not built.**

The generic profile approach test samples road overlap, retaining-wall tops, supports and nearby street lights. The May Road vault test samples the arch relative to the queried road and a hypothetical car envelope. None is continuous swept-volume, construction, actual road geometry, vegetation or building clearance proof. `tests/visual/peak-tram-may-road-bridge.html` is an isolated forced-WebGL view without roads and shows terrain-cut edge gaps; it is not a full game check. General ground queries still use the original DTM; track is not walkable or a working railway. The loop and track to the upper terminus are not yet installed.
