# Experimental full-length Peak Tram track

The standalone `tests/visual/peak-tram-track.html` renders paired 1.524 m-gauge rails, sleepers and track bed along both branches of the OSM trace. It is a **diagnostic preview, not the active game**. Two static car models are moved through an experimental counter-running cycle, with terminal dwells, a time slider and optional follow camera. It does not contain actual road bridges, cuts, station approaches, switch hardware, passengers or playable operating logic. Its translucent terrain is deliberately shown so buried or unsupported sections remain visible. It must not be mistaken for a finished railway.

The isolated `peak-tram-motion.js` measures the trial polylines in 3D and places the 33 m car on the chord between two samples, approximating slope and direction. It uses an analytic back-and-forth cycle, 6 m/s nominal speed and 12-second terminal dwells. Because the two trial branches measure approximately 1326.02 m and 1322.72 m in 3D, each is independently normalized to reach its endpoint; this is not a constant-length rope or a traction simulation. No acceleration, door, intermediate-stop or passenger logic is implemented. Car bodies do not articulate to follow curves, and collision/clearance at the passing loop, buildings, roads and stations is not verified. This code and cars are deliberately not integrated into the playable scene. Standalone forced-WebGL rendering and slider/follow controls were checked; this is not a full-game or WebGPU check.

`peak-tram-sample-profile.mjs` samples both branches at no more than 2 m along each OSM segment. Shared OSM nodes and common segments use shared variables. `peak-tram-joint-profile.py` uses numpy, scipy and OSQP to fit both routes simultaneously to DTM under sampled known-road elevation envelopes, a maximum gradient of tan(26°), an illustrative 0.012 per metre maximum change in grade, and the existing upper stub's estimated rail height. Curvature is a modelling choice, not an operating specification. `peak-tram-profile-data.js` is the checked-in result, not surveyed track. To reproduce, install numpy, scipy and osqp in a Python environment, then run `python3 tools/cities/hongkong/build-peak-tram-profile.py`; solver versions and tolerances can change numeric output. OSM trace © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.

`node tools/cities/hongkong/check-peak-tram-joint-profile.mjs` checks the closest road query with a 1.65 m lateral margin. At present it reports 42 known crossing samples per branch, no violations beyond 1 mm numerical tolerance, and six unknown samples per branch. This is **not a clearance check**: only one nearest road is returned, the 6.3 m vertical envelope is hypothetical, and actual road/bridge meshes, other levels, swept carriage envelope and path continuity are not tested. The unknown overlap labelled 蒲魯賢徑 (Brewin Path; earlier notes incorrectly called it Plunkett's Road) is not classified and is not constrained. The source `roads_minor_sw.json` identifies OSM way 156335538 as a residential asphalt way; its generated 8.5 m-wide ribbon approaches within roughly 4.15 m of the experimental track centre. Hiking accounts describe Brewin Path as part of the route that leaves the track after Magazine Gap Road ([Fitz](https://fitz.hk/sports/hiking/%E5%B7%A8%E6%9C%A8%E5%8F%83%E5%A4%A9%E7%BA%9C%E8%BB%8A%E5%BE%91%E4%B8%AD%E5%8D%80%E8%87%AA%E7%84%B6%E5%BE%91-%E5%B1%B1%E9%A0%82%E5%85%AC%E5%9C%92/)); these do not establish the crossing's exact clearance. Check actual road and track geometry before deciding whether the inferred road width or alignment needs correction. The trial track is up to 7.87 m below or 8.19 m above current terrain; the cuts, retaining walls and bridge supports do not exist. The tentative lower rail height is about 39.22 m, not a real measured terminus elevation. Upper alignment still assumes an estimated existing station level and the current station is too narrow for the provisional car model.

Do not activate this geometry until a defensible road/rail and station design, terrain openings, bridge structures, clearance against actual geometry and game collision/traffic tests are completed. The standalone preview was visually checked using forced WebGL; the full game and WebGPU have not been verified.
