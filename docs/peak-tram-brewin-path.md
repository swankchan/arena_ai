The trial alignment now reaches OSM node 26412146 (s≈397.7 m), passing the source OSM way 156335538, Brewin Path / 蒲魯賢徑. Its generated road was an 8.5 m two-way ribbon whose edge overlapped the railway. As an **estimated**, non-surveyed correction, its unchanged centreline and two-way topology now use a 4.8 m-wide ribbon throughout that generated way. This is a narrow road, not a claim of measured kerbs; whether this is an appropriate representation of the real way requires field and driving checks. No source OSM coordinates were changed. The traffic graph's two links still resolve to the rendered path, cars use a reduced lane-centre offset and may route over this corrected way, while buses no longer spawn on or enter a corrected road this narrow. There is no simulation of two large vehicles passing safely. Transitions to adjacent wider ways also need in-game inspection.

Original source data remain intact, and the correction fails closed if its source or previous override changes. Nearby buildings, trees and utility wires remain unverified.
