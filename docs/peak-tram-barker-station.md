# Barker Road station and crossing: scene-fit draft

This connects the estimated rail profile from s=1070 to s=1116 m, between the previously built approach and the new upper terminus approach. The model adds a two-rail track, sleepers, bed, local cuts, supports and a provisional thin deck over Plantation Road (road index 36109). Station artwork is an approximate 17 m stepped western-side platform with yellow edge, flat roof, posts, arch outlines, fan-pattern ironwork, station sign and warm night-emissive lamps. These are inferred dimensions and placement, **not a surveyed or complete architectural reconstruction**.

The [Antiquities Advisory Board appraisal](https://www.aab.gov.hk/filemanager/aab/common/historicbuilding/en/N26_Appraisal_En.pdf) documents the 1919 building's flat roof over track and platform, masonry buttresses, three arches with keystones and later ornamental ironwork, annex and red-brick walkway. The image at [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Peak_Tram_approaching_Barker_Road_Station.jpg) shows the track, level roof and arches; the [bridge image category](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Peak_Tram_bridge_at_Barker_Road) shows the road crossing. The annex, brick walkway, accurate carved details and real connections to surrounding streets remain missing. The model's narrow columns and exposed supports are not a reconstruction of the real masonry foundations.

An approximately 5 cm interval sample across the 3.3 m deck found the minimum deck-bottom to represented road height about 4.81 m. This is a diagnostic against the current generated road surface, not a design clearance or dynamic traffic test. The existing OSM road is represented as a tunnel and has pre-existing terrain opening rectangles: the isolated road-inclusive view reveals large cut edges/voids at the crossing, which still need terrain and bridge abutment work. Do not treat the current bridge as complete. Piers and columns were sampled against the road; nearby mapped street lamps were checked against an approximate track envelope. Full buildings, trees, vehicles, cables, slope-following car swept clearance, ground queries and runtime WebGPU remain unverified. The station steps and access are not implemented as walkable boarding.

The active rail geometry is now connected to the existing upper station at the profile centreline, but full-line playable operation is not implemented, and mechanical switches and terminal stopping/door clearance remain unresolved. See `peak-tram-summit-approach.md`.
