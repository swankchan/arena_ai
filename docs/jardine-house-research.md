# HK-B0037 — 怡和大廈 Jardine House

- [OSM way 42293714 v12](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/way/42293714/full.json) supplies the 14-point footprint, 178.54 m height and 53 levels. [Hong Kong Tourism Board](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/eng/place-to-go/travel.guide-jardine-house.html) states 52 storeys, 1973, architect James Kinoshita for Palmer & Turner; floor-count conventions are not combined.
- The [50th-anniversary design-spot record](https://2024.bodwinthecity.com/en/design-spots/jardine-house) reports 1,748 circular windows. In the asset-free rendition the four major faces have 48 visual rows of 11/7/11/7 apertures (1,728); this is a proportional visual approximation, **not** a claim to reproduce every original window. Window rims, glazing, joints and vertical positions are photo-directed, not measured.
- The exterior uses perforated metallic panels, circular recessed glazing and inner reveals. At ground level a recessed glazed lobby and transfer piers are approximated from [ground-level photographs](https://www.flickr.com/photos/18378305@N00/6411771823/). BaseHall, stairs, lobby interior and Central elevated walkways are not implemented, and the lobby is not a walk-through interior. Support arrangement and entry treatment are simplified.
- Nearby road surfaces sampled at 1 m were clear of authored wall colliders to 4.8 m. This is not a swept-body drive test. The model was inspected in a standalone forceWebGL view, not the full WebGPU game. DTM is not a verified architectural survey datum.
