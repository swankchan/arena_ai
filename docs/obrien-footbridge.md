# O'Brien Road footbridge (HF95) — partial reconstruction

The covered main route follows OpenStreetMap way 148784104 (14 nodes), with an approximate stair at way 148858993 and the short connecting branch at way 963983432. Research images and attribution are in `references/hongkong/immigration-tower-footbridge/`. The deck, covered roof, paving joints, yellow guidance strip, railings and night luminaires are modelled in geometry; the photographs are not game textures. Two approximate openings have been cut in the tower's first-floor facade, preserving a centre pier. The bridge is available to pedestrian height selection, not cars.

The absolute deck height of 12.5 m, 7.2 m width, stair profile and structural supports are **estimates**, not surveyed engineering data. OSM does not specify stair elevations. The pier placement skips positions near mapped road ribbons; the nearby Gloucester Road service ribbon (road 8995) was narrowed from the inferred 8.4 m to 7.0 m, retaining two lanes and its centreline, so it does not intersect the stair. This is an approximation, not surveyed kerb geometry.

Automated checks sample road clearance for the deck and staircase, and collider clearance for nearby traffic; they are discrete checks, not a guarantee of continuous clearance. A limited 1/F vestibule now provides a walkable floor at the bridge height, tile joints, metallic tactile strips, ceiling lights and an entrance sign. Its 20 × 9 m enclosure and interior finishes are provisional, inferred from the entrance view rather than a measured lobby plan. Its internal edges are temporarily walled; there is no finished link to Central Plaza, no interior stair or lift, and the full lobby is not modelled. The Wan Chai station connection, other branches, lifts and escalators are not yet modelled. Street furniture and full player/vehicle traversal, day/night appearance, terrain edges and WebGPU rendering have not been verified in the full game. Do not treat this as a complete or surveyed reconstruction.

The generic 9.6 m streetlight model would protrude through the new walkway or stairs at seven existing rendered lamp positions. Those seven are conservatively suppressed after the normal road/tram clearance overrides; source records are unchanged. The test checks each pole and arm footprint (with a small head margin). This does not identify their real heights, prove actual on-site conflicts, or replace their road illumination; a photo-led relocation or bridge-integrated lighting plan remains to be checked.

OSM way 148858993 is tagged `covered=yes`; its earlier uncovered model now has an estimated sloping metal-coloured canopy, posts, dark tread nosings and night luminaires. The POF stair/escalator photo is a general material reference only, not proof of this particular stair's geometry. The staircase arrangement, canopy profile and adjacent escalator remain unverified. The roof overhang was sampled against nearby mapped roads in addition to the previous stair clearance checks; those samples are not continuous collision validation.
