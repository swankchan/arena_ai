# 香港紅帆船 — visual reference

Research 2026-10-24. Reference previews only, not game textures; copyright remains with image owners, and redistribution permission has not been confirmed. Dates/operating details may change.

- `images/aqualuna-night.webp`: [KKday Aqua Luna cruise](https://www.kkday.com/en/product/22886): harbour-side image of dark curved wooden hull, raised stern, two decks, three unequal red battened sails and night-lit saloon. Photo metadata and lighting dates not verified.
- `images/aqualuna-deck.jpg`: [ThinkerTen](https://thinkerten.com/wordpress/2019/05/best-way-to-see-hong-kong-victoria-harbour-with-aqualuna-red-junk-boat/): foreground deck, dark rail, lifebuoy, sail spars and rigging; shows look and construction rather than measured dimensions.
- `images/aqualuna-side.jpg`: [Headout cruise](https://www.headout.com/aqua-luna-cruise-c-4864/): whole-boat side view of sail spacing and silhouette; photo preview may not be a current configuration.
- `images/dukling-dusk.webp`: [KKday Dukling](https://www.kkday.com/en/product/129026-dukling-harbour-cruise-hong-kong): different historic boat, for comparison of red illuminated sails; do not treat as Aqua Luna.

[Hong Kong government Film Promotion and Facilitation Section](https://fpf.ccidahk.gov.hk/en/location/detail.php?id=1920) lists Aqua Luna as 28 m, 2006, two decks, three decorative crimson sails; Aqua Luna II is 29 m and a different vessel. [Culture Trip](https://theculturetrip.com/asia/hong-kong/articles/the-story-of-hong-kongs-iconic-aqua-luna-red-sail-junk-boats) describes upper open deck, glazed lower saloon, motorised propulsion and decorative sails. [Discover Hong Kong](https://www.discoverhongkong.com/ca/plan/guided-tour/dukling-harbour-cruise.html) identifies Dukling as a restored former working Chinese junk, not the same vessel. These references inspired an unbranded model, not a verified replica.

Boat width, draft, mast and sail heights, layout, lighting and sailing course are approximations. Orange lifebuoys and lighting are indicative, not operational navigation equipment. The animated ellipse is fictional, not a real service route; land/road/ferry clearance tests are sampled and not navigational certification.
