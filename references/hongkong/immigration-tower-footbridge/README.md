# 柯布連道行人天橋 HF95 — research archive

The covered O'Brien Road footbridge links Immigration Tower first floor and Wan Chai MTR / Hennessy Road; this is **not** the separate uncovered link from the tower to Central Plaza. Searches and inspection 2026-10-24. Images are for research, not in-game textures; rights noted below. Neither image perspective nor OSM layer implies surveyed structural elevation or width.

## Geometry / topology

- [OSM way 148784104 v12 full](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/way/148784104/full.json), © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL: 14-node covered footway, bridge=yes, layer=1, from 22.2773724/114.1732880 to Immigration Tower node 1235363336 at 22.2796098/114.1730563. [Way 364266386 v5](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/way/364266386/full.json) is a level-1 indoor route through the building. Nodes do not specify a precise deck elevation or clear width.
- [POF accessibility guide](https://www.pof.org.hk/accessibility-search-detail.php?id=4007) describes two always-open glazed doorways divided by stone at the first-floor covered HF95 entrance, an unroofed connection to Central Plaza, and three interior flights. Do not block the openings with a collision wall or confuse it with the separate G/F entry.
- [FEHD](https://www.fehd.gov.hk/english/pleasant_environment/hawker/blackspots/WanChai.html) identifies O'Brien Road footbridge as connecting Wan Chai station and Immigration Tower. [Commons bridge category](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Footbridge_between_Immigration_Tower_and_Wan_Chai_Station) says the covered route crosses Gloucester, Jaffe, Lockhart and Hennessy Roads.
- Street approach escalators exist in several locations per [POF route guide](https://www.pof.org.hk/accessibility-search-detail.php?id=4005); positions and moving mechanism need separate research. A flat covered bridge without an accessible ground connection is incomplete.

Shows the bridge-side double entrance and glimpse of lit interior: dark sign fascia, stone-faced centre pier, metal/glass doors, silvery tactile warning paving, warm ceiling lights. The visible interior geometry is not sufficient to determine a floor plan.

Also describes a three-flight stair between G and 1/F, but does not give a floor plan.
