### Additional verified reference (2026-10-24)

- [whole-tower-2016.jpg](images/whole-tower-2016.jpg) — full south and east faces and roof, photographed 7 February 2016; Wikipedia user **Prosperity Horizons (formerly Exploringlife), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)**, [original file and licence](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Immigration_Tower_(revised).jpg). Search-result preview resized; no content change. The image confirms dark broad horizontal belts about one third and two thirds up the shaft, continuous small glass panels, and broad dark corner piers. The taller striped building and mast behind it are Central Plaza, not Immigration Tower. The harbour-facing billboard is not visible from this viewpoint.
