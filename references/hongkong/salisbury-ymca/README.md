The earlier project's claim of an all-pale podium and red wall is contradicted by these clearer views: the street frontage reads predominantly dark charcoal/blue-grey fine tile, with a brown/dark ground base; red is the YMCA logo, not a large wall. The upper tower cladding is light grey/ivory with varied medium-to-dark vertical inset panels; it must not be featureless white. These are photographic interpretations rather than verified material specifications. This is a 16-storey complex according to [Wikipedia history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YMCA_of_Hong_Kong); [YMCA official page](https://www.ymcahk.org.hk/thesalisbury/en/accommodation/index.html) confirms the podium garden, indoor swimming facilities and rooms. A [visitor report](https://sillycoupleblog.tw/hongkong-yautsimmong-accommodation-ymcaytm/) describes separate Salisbury Road and Middle Road entrances, and a three-level skylit lobby. No architectural plans or surveyed elevations were obtained. Precise dimensions, roof details, window occupancy at night and exact lighting remain estimates.
