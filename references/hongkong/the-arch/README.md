# 凱旋門 The Arch — visual research

Searches 2026-10-24: harbour and courtyard elevations, tower close-ups, podium and night view. Images are search previews saved for reference, not game textures. Copyright remains with the owners; permission to redistribute or publish these images has not been established. Watermarks retained.

- `images/cnn-courtyard.jpg`: [CNN article](https://www.cnn.com/style/article/hong-kong-skyscrapers-with-holes). Courtyard view: four red-brown residential wings, slim pale fins, tall rectangular void, projecting central glazed clubhouse. The courtyard-side upper centre looks substantially different from the harbour face.
- `images/superstock-harbour.webp`: [SuperStock photo](https://www.superstock.com/asset/china-hong-kong-west-kowloon-arch-building/1606-187404). Harbour-facing lower-angle view: copper/red vertical towers, green glazing, projecting curved aqua clubhouse facade, deep soffit and small circular upper opening. Orange Elements podium visible below; exact finishes and dimensions unconfirmed.
- `images/oneday-facade.jpg`: [OneDay Moon Tower](https://oneday.com.hk/en_US/buildings/the-arch). Close view of tower facade, balconies, clubhouse and arch soffit.
- `images/oneday-podium.jpg`: [OneDay Sky Tower](https://www.oneday.com.hk/en_US/buildings/the-arch-sky-tower-tower-1/). Close low-angle view of podium and facade; orange cladding relates to the wider Elements podium, not proof of the full estate geometry.

[Skyscraper Center](https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/building/the-arch/1086) lists 231 m and 65 physical floors; OSM tags four rings as 64 levels. [Cementaid](https://cementaid.com/project/the-arch-kowloon-station/) describes the clubhouse pool and connects towers at the 69th numbered floor; other sources give 62nd. [House730](https://www.house730.com/estate-13360/九龍站-凱旋門/) reports clubhouse 59–62/F: numbering conventions or descriptions differ. Do not infer surveyed storey elevations from these labels.

Materials, dimensions and lights are inferred from imagery, not verified specifications. The exact panel on the courtyard-facing crown, precise clubhouse footprint, entrance and podium levels remain uncertain. Road clearance, terrain and geometry tests are not a substitute for in-game WebGPU inspection or driving.
