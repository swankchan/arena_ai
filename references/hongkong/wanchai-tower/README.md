# 灣仔政府大樓 / Wanchai Tower — research

12 Harbour Road, not the adjacent Revenue or Immigration Towers. The Hong Kong government film-location page describes the lower floors as District Court, upper floors as government offices, and north and south exits with broad stepped forecourts. [Government site and official photographs](https://fpf.ccidahk.gov.hk/tc/location/detail.php?id=377). [CTBUH](https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/building/wan-chai-tower/21626) gives 136.5 m and 32 floors; [OSM way 27087041 v14](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/way/27087041/full.json) gives 137 m, 33 levels, outline and entrance nodes. OSM © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. Floors and geometrical details should not be mistaken for surveyed construction drawings.

Visible characteristics: pale solid outer piers with weathering; long dark recessed horizontal windows at lower levels; dark blue-grey fine grid curtain wall to upper portion; tall glazed entrance pavilion with sloping top, dark frames and bilingual letters. No measurements of wall setbacks, actual glass properties, lighting layout, terrain engineering or forecourt dimensions are claimed here.
