# Government House Open Day: official visitor guide (17 March 2019)

Source: [Chief Executive's Office, Guide for Visitors (PDF)](https://www.ceo.gov.hk/gh/pdf/Guide_for_Visitors_20190317_en.pdf). Retrieved as text 2026-10-24; this file preserves a short research extract, not the PDF or its images. Copyright remains with the source. Reference only; check rights before reuse.

## Relevant information

- Government House is a declared monument; its Open Day has been held since 1968.
- On 17 March 2019 visitors entered by the east gate on Upper Albert Road and followed a one-way route. A separate passage was available for wheelchair users. This describes that day's arrangement, not necessarily a permanent layout.
- Visitors could see the garden and landscaped area; over 30 species were mentioned, including azaleas, hydrangeas and a Rose of Venezuela tree recorded in the Register of Old and Valuable Trees.
- Docent services took place near the fountain in the back garden; the main entrance of the main building had displays.
