# Government House — source notes

Research notes, not full copies of the linked documents; recorded 2026-10-24.

- [Chief Executive's Office](https://www.ceo.gov.hk/gh/eng/house.html): official residence, grounds approximately 24,000 m², front elevation faces south toward the Peak, north side overlooks former Central Government Offices. Declared monument.
- [Antiquities and Monuments Office](https://www.amo.gov.hk/en/historic-buildings/monuments/hong-kong-island/monuments_59/index.html): built 1855, east wing added in 1889, Japanese-occupation modifications including central tower and roof; the page includes photos and heritage context.
- [Mapped building footprint](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/way/42258038/full.json): OSM way 42258038 v14, © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Embedded in the scene's `government-house-architecture.js`.
- [OSM barrier 1294918105](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/1294918105): mapped barrier node near a service road. This is **not confirmed** to be the ceremonial entrance in the photograph.
- Photographs in this folder show the tower's shallow arched openings, hooded openings, latticed lower windows and dark tiled hipped roof; the north facade has a series of large arches. Details and dimensions inferred from photographs are not measured drawings.
- Ground datum follows existing terrain sampling; no source here confirms surveyed elevations, exact gate-lodge footprints, full garden layout or indoor floor plans.

## Grounds boundary (2026-10-24)

- [Sophie, Albert Path, 2024](https://note.com/sophie_bl_hk/n/n23593026cff9): identifies the secured boundary behind the Albert Path sign as Government House; archived photo in this folder shows pale pierced wall and dark spear-topped railings. This differs from the granite gate-wall photo, so materials cannot be assumed uniform along the perimeter.
- [OpenStreetMap service way 114282491](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/114282491), version 3, traverses gate node 1294918105 and continues around the grounds. The gate's ceremonial role and exact lodge locations remain unverified. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
- [CEO overview](https://www.ceo.gov.hk/gh/eng/house.html): grounds approximately 24,000 m², between Upper and Lower Albert Roads. This is not a surveyed perimeter plan. The existing two short fence sections at the guessed gate lodges are illustrative, not evidence of the whole property boundary.
