# Victoria Park, Causeway Bay — reusable visual references

Image files here are research references, not textures included in the scene. The downloaded versions are resized search previews; the linked Commons pages provide the original and full licensing details.

- `bandstand-chong-fat-2009.jpg`: Chong Fat, 14 March 2009, [HK VictoriaPark Bandstand.JPG](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:HK_VictoriaPark_Bandstand.JPG), [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Resized preview. Photo shows two-peaked white tensile canopy, thin posts and guy cables, rough grey stone rear wall, dark raised stage, spotlights and pale paved forecourt. The previous octagonal Victorian gazebo was wrong for this Hong Kong site. The photo predates the present; dimensions and orientation must not be inferred as surveyed.
- `basketball-mk2010-2011.jpg`: Mk2010, 2 February 2011, [Basketball courts of the Victoria Park, Hong Kong.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Basketball_courts_of_the_Victoria_Park,_Hong_Kong.jpg), [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Resized preview. Colour and court fencing reference; age limits current-colour certainty.

Other primary visual sources to revisit: [2018 overview, Wpcpey, CC BY-SA 4.0](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Victoria_Park_Overview_201810.jpg), [2023 tennis and swimming, Nath87, CC BY-SA 4.0](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Victoria_Park_Tennis_and_Swimming.jpg), [2017 football pitches, YEUNGAN ginlwims, CC BY-SA 4.0](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:HK_CWB_%E9%8A%85%E9%91%BC%E7%81%A3_Causeway_Bay_%E7%B6%AD%E5%A4%9A%E5%88%A9%E4%BA%9E%E5%85%AC%E5%9C%92_Victoria_Park_football_pitches_July_2017_IX1_02.jpg), [Commons model-boat-pool category](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Victoria_Park_Model_Boat_Pool), [government landscape gallery](https://www.greening.gov.hk/en/greening-landscape/landscape-corner/index_id_58.html) and [film location gallery](https://fpf.ccidahk.gov.hk/en/location/detail.php?id=39). These linked images are not all saved here; check each licence before copying.

Avoid conflating Victoria Park with Hong Kong Park, Victoria Peak Garden, or the Bermuda/London parks of the same name. The Commons photo titled “Soccer Fields At Victoria Park (209711927)” carries coordinates far from Causeway Bay; do not use it as positional evidence without corroboration.
