## Further sources and modelling cautions

- [Official history](https://www.thepeak.com.hk/en/heritage/history-of-the-peak-tram) gives 1,278 m, 25.7° maximum incline, and terminus elevations of 33 m and 396 m; other official publications differ. None is a surveyed height profile.
- The [2022 opening release](https://www.thepeak.com.hk/sites/peak/files/2022-08/Announce%20Launch%20Date%20of%206th%20generation%20Peak%20Tram_ENG_FINAL.pdf) describes the extended Central Terminus and level-access platform. The [artwork announcement](https://www.thepeak.com.hk/sites/peak/files/2022-08/Peak%20Tram%20Unveils%20Stunning%20Public%20Artwork%20-%20Eye%20of%20Infinity%20by%20Lindy%20Lee_1.pdf) describes Lindy Lee's illuminated perforated bronze Eye of Infinity at the entrance.
- The government [Barker Road heritage appraisal](https://www.aab.gov.hk/filemanager/aab/common/historicbuilding/en/N26_Appraisal_En.pdf) describes stone retaining structure, flat concrete roof, colonnade, chamfered columns, round arches and ironwork. [Commons Barker Road photographs](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Barker_Road_(Peak_Tram)) offer further views; individual licences and dates must be checked before copying.
- [Gwulo's MacDonnell Road bridge photo index](https://gwulo.com/node/42813/photos) includes [a 1989 view](https://gwulo.com/media/51998) of the road over the track and [a 2010 view](https://gwulo.com/media/31317). Licence is not established; linked only. These are historical views, not a measured geometry or proof of the post-upgrade bridge. [Checkerboard Hill's 2011 account](https://www.checkerboardhill.com/2011/02/the-peak-tram-trainspotters-edition/) describes the stone arches and a small stepped-platform shelter; [MacDonnell Road stop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacDonnell_Road_stop) records the single western platform and the road bridge at its downhill end. The existing flat underpass soffit is not a stone arch.
- [Passing-loop photographs](https://www.checkerboardhill.com/2019/07/hong-kong-peak-tram-passing-loop-rails/) are linked only; their reuse licence has not been established. They are historical and should not be assumed to show the current rebuilt track.
- The repository's `docs/peak-tram-research.md` and two diagnostic scripts document the OSM trace, steep DEM artefacts and existing road crossings. A terrain-draped track or simply overlapping roads is not an acceptable solution. Establish a plausible continuous vertical profile, bridge and underpass envelopes, station access and road continuity before laying the full route; audit actual geometry, traffic and day/night appearance in the game. DEM and OSM do not establish surveyed engineering elevations.
