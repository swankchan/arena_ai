# Central Terminus reference photographs

These are research references, not game textures. The contemporary street entrance is not the same location as the present boarding platform.

- `entrance-2022.png`: [The Peak Tram Entrance after renovation in 2022](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Peak_Tram_Entrance_after_renovation_in_2022.png), 漫遊香港 A walk around Hong Kong, October 2022, [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/); unmodified image retrieved through image search. Shows the black bronze Eye of Infinity, reflecting pool and curved pale-grey surround, grey stone entrance and red paving, glazed dark entry, chrome supports and rounded-corner grey St John's Building facade. Note the raised roadway at left and greenery above the entrance.
- `alighting-platform-2022-thumbnail.jpg`: [Peak Tram Central Terminus alighting platform 27-08-2022](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Peak_Tram_Central_Terminus_alighting_platform_27-08-2022.jpg), LN9267, 27 August 2022, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/); small 120 px Wikimedia thumbnail as retrieved through image search, not the full-resolution original. Its documented camera location is 22.277201, 114.158628; higher resolution available at the linked source.
